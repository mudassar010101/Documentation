Integrations

Integrations connect NexCognit to the tools your business already uses, so your agent can sync leads, book meetings, and send messages for you.

Integrations overview

The top of the page shows how many apps you have connected.

Available integrations

  • Zoho CRM: Syncs every lead and keeps its stage up to date automatically.
  • Google Calendar: Lets your agent book demos and meetings in your calendar.
  • Gmail: Sends follow-up and nurture emails from your own email address.
  • Instagram: Lets your agent send replies to Instagram messages.
  • Facebook Page: Lets your agent post and reply on your Facebook Page.

Connect or disconnect an app

  • Click Connect on an app's card, then sign in to that app on its own page.
  • Connected apps show Connected with a green tick.
  • Click Disconnect on a connected app to remove it at any time.

You always sign in on the app's own page. NexCognit never sees your passwords.

Your API keys

Some features use other services, for example ElevenLabs for voice. If a feature asks for a key, click Add key to add it here. Keys are stored securely, and only the last four characters are ever shown.

You only need this section when a feature asks for a key.

Things to know

  • Connect Zoho CRM to complete the last step of your setup.
  • See the Zoho CRM and Google Calendar guides in the sidebar for step-by-step help.
  • Never share your keys or passwords with anyone.

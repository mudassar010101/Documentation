Plan & credits

The Plan & credits page shows your current plan, how many AI credits you have left, and details about your workspace.

Plan & credits overview

Open Plan & credits from the bottom of the sidebar, under Account.

Current plan and credit balance

  • Current plan: The name of the plan you are on, for example Trial.
  • Credit balance: How many credits you have left out of your total.
  • Progress bar: Shows the percentage of credits you have left.
  • Upgrade or top up: Move to a bigger plan or add more credits.
  • Compare plans: See the available plans side by side.

How credits work

Your AI agent uses credits as it works, for example when it replies to customers. Your plan's credits renew with each billing period.

If your credits reach zero, your AI agent stops replying to customers. Your data and settings stay safe, and the agent starts working again once you add more credits.

Workspace

  • Name: The name of your workspace.
  • Workspace ID: A unique code for your workspace. Our support team may ask for it when you contact us.
  • How you sign in: The account you use to sign in to NexCognit.

Questions about billing

If you need help with invoices, plan changes, or custom volumes, use one of the buttons in the Questions about billing? box:

  • Contact us: Send a message to our team.
  • Book a call: Schedule a call with our team.

Things to know

  • Check your credit balance regularly so your AI agent does not stop replying.
  • Top up or upgrade before your credits run low, especially during busy periods.

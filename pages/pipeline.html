Pipeline

The Pipeline page shows your leads as cards on a board, grouped by how ready they are to buy. It gives you a simple picture of where every deal stands.

Pipeline overview

Each column is a stage. Each card in a column is one lead.

Pipeline stages

  • New: Just arrived, not scored yet.
  • Warm: Interested, but needs more nurturing.
  • Hot: Ready to buy.
  • Cold: Low interest for now.

The number at the top of each column shows how many leads are in that stage.

What each card shows

  • Name: The customer's name, or Website visitor if the name is not known yet.
  • Stage label: The lead's current stage, with a small arrow to open the stage menu.
  • Channel icon and time: Where the lead came from and when they were last active.

Move a lead to another stage

As a deal moves forward, you can change its stage in two ways:

  • Drag and drop: Drag the card into another column.
  • Stage menu: Click the small arrow next to the stage label on the card and choose a new stage.

Things to know

  • Your AI agent places leads in a stage automatically, and you can move them yourself at any time.
  • Keep an eye on the Hot column so ready buyers are contacted quickly.
  • Use the Pipeline for a quick visual check, and the Leads page when you need more details.

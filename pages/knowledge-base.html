Knowledge base

The Knowledge base is the information your agent searches before every reply. Add your website, FAQs, price lists, and guides so your agent answers like your best salesperson.

Knowledge base overview

At the top right you can see how many files and links you have added, and how much searchable information your agent has.

Train your agent and Knowledge score

The Train your agent bar shows three steps: Data added, Agent learning, and Ready to assist you. When all three are done, your agent has learned what you added.

The Knowledge score shows how complete your knowledge base is. It checks for:

  • Your website
  • Company profile, menu, or price list
  • FAQs or policies
  • Products and prices
  • Your agent's instructions

Items with a green tick are done. Use the button under the list to add what is missing.

Import from your website

NexCognit reads the page you enter and the pages it links to, then keeps the text for your agent.

  1. Enter the web address in Page address.
  2. Optionally, give it a name in Name, for example "Pricing page".
  3. Click Import.

Upload documents

Drag files into the box, or click it to choose files from your computer. You can upload PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, text, or Markdown files, up to 20 MB each.

For spreadsheets, a header row at the top helps your agent understand the data.

Products & prices

Add what you sell and what it costs, so your agent can quote prices and offer products to customers.

  • Add product: Add products one by one.
  • Import sheet: Add many products at once from a spreadsheet.

After adding products, sync them so your agent knows about them.

Your added content

  • Documents: Lists the files you have uploaded.
  • Website & imported content: Lists the websites you have imported, how many pages were read, and whether your agent has Learned them. Use Re-import after you update your website, or the edit and delete buttons to change or remove it.

Test your knowledge base

Type a question the way a customer would, for example "How much does the starter plan cost?", and click Search. You will see which information your agent would use to answer.

Things to know

  • The number of websites and documents you can add depends on your plan. The limit is shown under each box.
  • Re-import your website whenever you change it, so your agent always has the latest information.
  • Use Test your knowledge base to check answers before customers ask.
